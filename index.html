About the system

A dependable paper trail for every legislative action

The Legislative Agenda & Calendar Management System gives the Sangguniang Panlungsod a shared view of priorities, readings, schedules, meeting readiness, and statutory action windows.

Each item keeps its context as it moves from filing and priority review through calendar scheduling, deliberation, transmittal, and follow-up.
